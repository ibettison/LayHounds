## Method

- Each historical day is treated as one replay session, processed in market-time order.
- Stakes tested: £0.05, £0.50, £1.00, £1.50, £2.00.
- Stop-win values are scaled by stake: x10, x20, x40, x80.
- Stop-loss values are scaled by stake: x20, x40, x80, x160.
- Max liability cap is scaled by stake: x100.
- Other settings: 2 favourites, strict favourite-risk guard, max recovery L3, max 200 races.
- Ranking priority: positive total P&L, positive days, stopped-win days, total P&L, fewer stopped-loss days, fewer busts.
